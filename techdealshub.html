About Us

Football Hub is an independent football shopping and discovery website. We help football fans explore jerseys, boots, training clothing, equipment, accessories and ticket opportunities from external retailers.

Football Hub does not sell products or issue match tickets directly. Product information, prices, availability, delivery terms and ticket conditions are determined by the relevant retailer or ticket provider. Please confirm all details on the provider's website before purchasing.

Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on Football Hub may be affiliate links. If you follow a qualifying affiliate link and make a purchase, Football Hub may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

Not every link on this website is necessarily an affiliate link. Some links lead to ordinary product searches or external websites. The presence of a link does not guarantee that Football Hub receives a commission.

Retailers are responsible for their own products, prices, availability, delivery, returns and customer service. Please review the retailer's terms before completing an order.

Privacy Policy

Football Hub aims to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary to operate this website. The site's product search and category filters run in your browser.

Saved products

If you save a product, the saved product identifiers may be stored in your browser's local storage so they can remain available on your device. You can remove saved products using the saved-products button or clear this website's local storage in your browser settings.

Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics is not activated in this file until the website owner adds a valid GA4 Measurement ID. If analytics or other third-party services are enabled later, those services may process information according to their own policies and the site's applicable consent requirements.

External websites

When you follow a link to a retailer or ticket provider, you leave Football Hub. The external provider's privacy policy and terms then apply. We recommend reviewing those policies before sharing personal information or making a purchase.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the website changes. Please revisit this section for the latest version.

Contact

For product questions, order status, ticket delivery, refunds or returns, please contact the retailer or ticket provider from which you are purchasing. Football Hub does not process orders or issue tickets.
