Design & Build

The iPhone 17 Pro Max features a premium titanium-inspired design with a refined aluminum frame, advanced durability, and a sleek modern appearance. Its lightweight construction provides a comfortable grip while maintaining professional-level strength.

The device includes a redesigned camera system, precision-crafted buttons, improved heat management, and enhanced protection against everyday use.

  • Ultra-thin and durable frame
  • Advanced thermal management system
  • Improved water and dust resistance
  • Available in multiple premium colors
